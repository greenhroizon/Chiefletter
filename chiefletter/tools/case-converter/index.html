Text case converter

Convert text to common programming and writing cases.

Convert a block of text to common writing and programming conventions. The tool supports uppercase, lowercase, title case, sentence case, camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case. It splits programming cases at punctuation and whitespace.

Use it here

Text case converter

Runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to Chiefletter.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste the words you want to convert.
  2. Choose the target case from the menu.
  3. Run the converter and review names, acronyms, and proper nouns before copying the result.

Use cases

  • Rename a variable to match a project convention.
  • Convert a heading into a lowercase URL-friendly phrase.
  • Standardize short labels in a spreadsheet or content list.

Notes and limits

Automatic title and sentence casing cannot know which brand names or technical acronyms must stay capitalized. Review the output instead of applying it blindly to a final document.

Frequently asked questions

Does camelCase preserve punctuation?

No. Punctuation separates words and is removed when the tool joins them in camelCase.

How does title case work?

It capitalizes letters after whitespace. It does not apply a publisher-specific style guide.

Is my source text saved?

No. Conversion happens in the page and the tool does not store your input.
