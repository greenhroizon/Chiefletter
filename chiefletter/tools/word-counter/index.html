Word and character counter

Count words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs.

Count words and characters while you edit. The tool also reports characters without spaces, sentence-ending punctuation, and paragraphs. Word counts use runs of letters or numbers and allow internal apostrophes, hyphens, and underscores.

Use it here

Word and character counter

Runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to Chiefletter.

How to use this tool

  1. Paste or write a draft in the text area.
  2. Read the live counts below the field. They update as you type.
  3. Compare characters with and without spaces when a form sets a strict limit.

Use cases

  • Check an application answer against a word limit.
  • Estimate the length of a headline, social post, or product description.
  • Measure a draft before sending it to an editor.

Notes and limits

Different publishers define words, sentences, and characters in different ways. Treat these values as a quick estimate and use the destination’s own counter for final submission.

Frequently asked questions

Do spaces count as characters?

The tool reports both total characters and characters with spaces removed. Line breaks count in the total.

How are words counted?

A word is a sequence of letters or numbers. Apostrophes, hyphens, and underscores inside a word stay together.

Does the counter send my draft anywhere?

No. Counts are calculated in the browser and the draft is not saved by this page.
