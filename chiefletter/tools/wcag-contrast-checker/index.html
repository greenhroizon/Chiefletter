Text contrast checker

Check foreground and background colors against WCAG contrast ratios.

Calculate the WCAG relative luminance contrast ratio for two sRGB colors and compare the result with common AA text thresholds. The ratio is a numeric check, not a full accessibility review.

Text contrast checker

Runs in your browser. Your input is not sent to Chiefletter.

How to use this tool

  1. Choose a foreground text color and background color.
  2. Check the contrast ratio and the displayed AA results.
  3. Test the colors in context, including hover, focus, disabled, and error states.

Use cases

  • Review text colors in a design system.
  • Check a button label against its fill color.
  • Compare alternate colors before a visual accessibility review.

Notes and limits

WCAG thresholds depend on text size and weight. This tool checks 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. It does not assess non-text contrast, meaning, or the final rendered colors after opacity and overlays.

Frequently asked questions

What ratio passes normal text AA?

A contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 is the common WCAG AA threshold for normal text.

What counts as large text?

WCAG defines large text by size and weight. Confirm the exact criterion for your design and conformance target.

Does passing this check make a page accessible?

No. It checks one color pair only. Keyboard access, semantics, focus, and other requirements still need review.
