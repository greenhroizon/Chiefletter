Cookie policy

This page describes the technologies used by Chiefletter to display its content.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a website or service may store in a browser. Similar technologies can store or access information on a device. They may support site functions, remember preferences, measure use, or personalize advertising.

Resources loaded by this site

Chiefletter loads typefaces from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash image services. Your browser makes requests to those providers to display the site. YouTube videos are linked externally and are not embedded on Chiefletter pages. Those providers have their own privacy practices.

Analytics and advertising

Chiefletter currently uses no analytics or advertising code and does not intentionally set first-party analytics or advertising cookies. If this changes, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect the services, technologies, and choices that apply. Google has separate consent management requirements for publishers serving personalized ads in certain regions; publishers should review current guidance before adding ads.

Browser controls

Most browsers let you review, block, or delete cookies. Blocking some technologies may affect the behavior of third-party services you choose to visit. Browser controls do not replace consent or choice mechanisms required by law.
