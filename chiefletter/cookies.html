Cookie policy

This page describes the technologies used by Chiefletter to display its content.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a website or service may store in a browser. Similar technologies can store or access information on a device. They may support site functions, remember preferences, measure use, or personalize advertising. Article checklists and saved notes use local storage in your browser. Those notes stay on your device and are separate from Google Analytics.

Resources loaded by this site

Chiefletter loads typefaces from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash image services. Your browser makes requests to those providers to display the site. YouTube videos are linked externally and are not embedded on Chiefletter pages. Those providers have their own privacy practices.

Analytics and advertising

Chiefletter loads Google Analytics through the Google tag with measurement ID G-KL069686WS and also loads Google AdSense. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure page use, subject to tag settings and Google’s terms. Ad display depends on settings in the connected AdSense account. Manage cookies through your browser and review Google’s own privacy controls. Consent requirements depend on your location and the way the services are configured.

Browser controls

Most browsers let you review, block, or delete cookies. Blocking some technologies may affect the behavior of third-party services you choose to visit. Browser controls do not replace consent or choice mechanisms required by law.
