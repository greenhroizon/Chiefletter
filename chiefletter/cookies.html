Cookie policy

The current starter site does not intentionally set first-party analytics, advertising, or preference cookies. This must be checked against the final host and every service added before publication.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website or service can store in a browser. Similar technologies may store or access information on a device. They can support basic site functions, remember preferences, measure use, or personalize advertising.

Third-party requests

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and images from Unsplash, and links to YouTube. These services may use their own technologies when your browser contacts them. We do not embed YouTube videos in the starter version. Review current provider documentation and browser behavior before launch, as third-party practices may change.

Advertising and analytics

No advertising or analytics service is connected in this version. If the publisher adds AdSense, analytics, or another service, this policy must explain the categories and purposes of technologies used, provide the controls and consent mechanism required for the audiences and regions served, and identify how users can change their choices.

Browser controls

Most browsers let you review, block, or delete cookies. Blocking some technologies may affect the features of a site. Browser controls do not replace any consent or choice mechanism required by law.

See our Privacy Policy for more information about the data practices that must be confirmed before launch.
