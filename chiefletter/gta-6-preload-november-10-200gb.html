GTA VI pre-load opens November 10 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a download of up to 212GB

Rockstar confirmed the pre-load date and size ahead of the November 19 launch.

By ChiefLetter StaffPublished Nov 3, 2026Updated Nov 12, 20263 min read

Key points

  • Pre-loading opened November 10: at midnight local time on PS5 and at 12 a.m. GMT globally on Xbox Series X|S.
  • Rockstar says the game needs up to 212GB, so players should clear space in advance.
  • The game launches November 19, 2026.
  • Players who chose the code-in-the-box option will see boxed codes on shelves from November 12.

Rockstar confirmed that Grand Theft Auto VI pre-loading unlocks on November 10 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The file is large: Rockstar's guidance puts it at up to 212GB, and the company advises players to clear space on their drives before installing. The game itself launches on November 19, 2026.

Pre-load windows exist so that players can download a game ahead of release and play the moment it unlocks. For a title of this scale, that matters more than usual.

When pre-loading starts

On PS5, pre-loads begin at midnight local time on November 10. On Xbox Series X|S, they begin globally at 12 a.m. GMT. That means players in different time zones see the download become available at different clock times, so anyone who wants to start as soon as it opens should check their console's local time.

Digital buyers can pre-load a week before launch. Players who chose the code-in-the-box option for a physical edition will find those codes on retail shelves starting November 12, after which they can redeem them and start the download too.

Preparing your console

A download of this size can take hours on slower connections, and on busy networks it can take longer. A few practical steps help:

  • Check free space first. Up to 212GB is a large share of a standard console drive, and you may need to uninstall other games or attach external or expansion storage.
  • Use a wired connection if you can. Ethernet is more stable than Wi-Fi for multi-hour downloads.
  • Keep the console in rest mode with downloads enabled so the transfer can continue overnight.
  • Install any day-one patch as soon as it is available, since patches can add further to the total.
  • Leave a little headroom beyond the stated size. Installs often need temporary space during unpacking.

Why the size is so big

Open-world games with high-resolution textures, large amounts of audio and extensive voice work take up a lot of storage, and 200GB-class installs have become more common this console generation. A game that is expected to be one of the biggest releases of the year has strong reasons to ship its highest-quality assets on the disc or in the download rather than compress them aggressively.

Storage has become a real purchase consideration for console owners. With several large games installed, many players now need to juggle libraries or buy extra drives.

Note on the file size

Earlier coverage, including this site's original report, cited a 200GB download. Rockstar's confirmed figure is up to 212GB, and we have updated this article to match. The exact size on your console may vary slightly by platform.

What to watch next

  • Launch day server load and any early patch notes.
  • Whether the download size changes with the first update.
  • How console storage and platform stores handle a surge in demand around release.
