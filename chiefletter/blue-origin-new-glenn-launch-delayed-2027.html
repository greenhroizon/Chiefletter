Blue Origin pushes New Glenn launch into 2027

The delay moves the next milestone for Jeff Bezos's rocket company into next year.

By ChiefLetter Editorial TeamPublished November 6, 20262 min read

Blue Origin has pushed a New Glenn launch into 2027, according to Silicon UK. The schedule slip leaves the heavy-lift rocket's next mission for the new year.

New Glenn is central to Blue Origin's plans to compete in commercial launch and national security missions, so timing matters to customers weighing launch providers.

Rocket schedules often slip, and a move into 2027 means customers planning launches must keep alternatives open. Heavy-lift rockets are scarce, so every delay reshapes the competitive picture.

New Glenn matters for large satellite constellations and government payloads. The longer the wait, the more business can go to rivals that already fly regularly.

Watch for updated launch windows, customer announcements and any statement on what drove the change.
