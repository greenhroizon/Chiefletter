Blue Origin pushes New Glenn launch into 2027

The delay moves the next milestone for Jeff Bezos's rocket company into next year.

Blue Origin has pushed a New Glenn launch into 2027, according to Silicon UK. The schedule slip leaves the heavy-lift rocket's next mission for the new year.

New Glenn is central to Blue Origin's plans to compete in commercial launch and national security missions, so timing matters to customers weighing launch providers.
