Hongqiao Tech Week debuts in Shanghai November 20-22

A new festival aims to link global technology companies with China's industrial ecosystem.

By ChiefLetter StaffPublished Nov 11, 2026Updated Nov 12, 20263 min read

Key points

  • The first Hongqiao Tech Week runs November 20 to 22 at the National Exhibition and Convention Center in Shanghai.
  • Organized by Informa Markets China, it covers AI, semiconductors, biopharmaceuticals, autonomous driving and new energy.
  • Six content stages, startup pitches, an AI application competition and site tours are planned.
  • Theme: "Where Global Tech Ambition Meets Chinese Scale."

Informa Markets China will hold the first Hongqiao Tech Week from November 20 to 22 at the National Exhibition and Convention Center in Shanghai. The event spotlights semiconductors, biopharmaceuticals, autonomous driving, new energy and AI, with the stated aim of connecting international participants with the Chinese market.

Its theme is "Where Global Tech Ambition Meets Chinese Scale." The organizers describe the target sectors as a "trillion-yuan industrial ecosystem" and say the event is intended to help global enterprises, business leaders, startups, investors and technology solution providers unlock growth opportunities and build China-focused partnerships.

Who is behind it

Informa Markets China, part of the global events group, is the organizer. The Shanghai Hongqiao International Central Business District Administrative Committee supports the event and describes it as a platform for technology collaboration in the Yangtze River Delta. A press conference was held in London on June 8, 2026, a sign that organizers are courting international attendees as well as domestic ones.

What is on the programme

The organizers describe six thematic content stages:

  • AI
  • Future Mobility and Energy
  • Digital Infrastructure
  • Industry 4.0
  • Life Sciences and Health
  • Startup

Other planned elements include startup roadshows and pitch showcases, augmented reality experiences, founder and CEO luncheons, an AI Application Innovation Competition and Awards, and talks by industry leaders. The programme also includes cultural and sensory features such as taste, music and art, fashion and sports, an unusual addition to a technology event.

Organizers have also announced VIP site tours around the Yangtze River Delta featuring companies including Huawei, Anta, Netease, Midea and iFlytek, giving international visitors a look at how large Chinese firms operate. The release does not name specific co-located conferences, and it does not give expected exhibitor or visitor numbers, ticket pricing or registration details, so anyone planning to attend should check the official channels.

Why it matters

Industry events have been shifting eastward as technology activity grows in China, and trade-show organizers have been opening new events to capture that demand. A debut event in Shanghai's Hongqiao district, which is already a major transport and business hub, fits that pattern.

The timing is also notable. Cross-border technology activity is more constrained than it once was, with governments scrutinizing foreign investment and data flows. This week, for example, China blocked SoftBank's acquisition of ABB's robotics business in the country. Events like Hongqiao Tech Week sit in the middle of that tension, offering a structured setting for deals and partnerships while the rules around them continue to change.

Practical notes for visitors

  • Check visa and entry requirements well ahead of travel.
  • Confirm registration and any invitation-only elements, such as networking receptions and luncheons, directly with the organizers.
  • Allow time for transport to the venue and for Shanghai's busy conference season.
  • If you plan to do business in China, take legal advice on regulations covering data, foreign investment and technology transfer.

What to watch next

  • Attendance and exhibitor numbers once the organizers publish them.
  • Which international companies take part and which deals are announced.
  • Whether the event becomes an annual fixture.
