Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

Scope and owner details

This policy describes the static ChiefLetter site supplied in the project. The owner must add the publisher’s legal identity, a verified privacy contact, the operating jurisdiction and any applicable privacy-law disclosures before launch. The project currently lists contact@chiefletter.com; verify the inbox before relying on it.

Information and server logs

Web hosting may process technical information such as IP address, request time, browser and device details, and pages requested in routine server logs. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include. Confirm the hosting provider, log retention and any analytics tools actually enabled; this site build does not add an analytics service.

Advertising and Google AdSense

The site includes the Google AdSense loader using the publisher ID supplied by the owner. Loading the script can allow Google and its partners to process information, use cookies or similar technologies, measure ads and personalize advertising where permitted and configured. The owner must review Google’s publisher policies, configure consent and privacy controls for the audiences and regions served, and update this notice to match the actual settings before serving ads.

Cookies and choices

Browser controls can limit or remove cookies, though some site or advertising features may not work as intended. Where law requires consent before non-essential storage or personalized advertising, the publisher must implement an appropriate consent mechanism before enabling those features.

Sharing, retention and rights

Service providers may process information to host or operate the site. The publisher should document retention periods, applicable access/deletion/objection rights, and the process for handling requests under the laws that apply. Do not treat this template as a statement that a particular request process or legal basis has been configured.

Children and changes

The site is not intended to collect personal information from children. The owner should assess any child-directed content or audience before launch. We may revise this policy when practices change and will update the date.

Contact

Privacy inquiries: contact@chiefletter.com (project-configured address; owner must verify it).
