Privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026.

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit ChiefLetter (the "Site"), how it is used and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Usage information. Standard data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and referring page, collected automatically.
  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message.

We do not require you to create an account and we do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information.

Cookies and similar technologies

We and our partners use cookies, pixels and similar technologies to operate the Site, measure traffic and show advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect how some features work.

Advertising and third parties

We display advertisements provided by third-party advertising networks. These partners may collect information such as your IP address, device identifiers and browsing activity on this and other sites in order to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We also use analytics and audience measurement tools, including pixels from content-recommendation partners. These parties have their own privacy policies, and we do not control their practices.

Depending on where you live, you may be able to opt out of personalised advertising through your browser, device settings or industry opt-out tools.

How we use information

  • To operate, secure and improve the Site.
  • To understand which content readers find useful.
  • To display and measure advertising.
  • To respond to your messages.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing, including under the GDPR, UK GDPR and California privacy laws. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above and use reasonable measures to protect it. No online service is completely secure.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@chiefletter.com.
