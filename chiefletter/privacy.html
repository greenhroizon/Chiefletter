Privacy policy

This page describes the current starter site's data practices. The publisher must review and update it to match the final hosting, analytics, newsletter, advertising, and contact services before launch.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Who operates this site

Plaintext Journal is a publication name used for this website. Before launch, replace this paragraph with the publisher's legal name, postal address or registered business details where required, country, and a working privacy contact. The site is not ready for public operation until those details are accurate.

Information collected by this starter site

The current static pages do not include an account system, comments, connected newsletter service, or working contact form. The newsletter form is a non-functional demonstration and does not transmit or store the email address entered. Do not enable collection without updating this policy and implementing suitable safeguards.

The hosting provider may receive technical information such as IP address, request time, browser and device information, and pages requested in server logs. Confirm the actual logs, retention period, processors, and hosting region with the selected host and replace this description as needed.

Third-party services and images

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and article images are loaded from Unsplash image CDN. Those services may receive technical request data when your browser loads their resources. YouTube links open YouTube search pages; this site does not embed video players. Review current third-party terms and privacy information before launch and disclose any additional services you add, including analytics, advertising, social widgets, or form providers.

Advertising and cookies

No advertising tag, analytics tool, or cookie consent platform is connected in this starter. If advertising such as Google AdSense is added, update this policy to explain the data and cookies used, configure consent and disclosures required for the audiences and regions served, and link to Google's current information. Ad approval is not guaranteed by publishing this policy.

How information may be used

If the publisher later receives messages through a contact route, the information will be used to respond, address corrections or rights claims, protect the site, and meet legal obligations. State the actual legal basis, retention periods, processors, and sharing practices applicable to the final operation of the site.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent. Contact the publisher using the real privacy contact added above. The publisher must add any region-specific disclosures, complaint authority details, and procedures that apply.

Children's privacy and security

This publication is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The publisher should update this section if the audience or services change. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be selected for the actual hosting and data practices; no internet transmission or storage method can be described as perfectly secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this notice when the site or its services change. The revision date above should reflect the latest substantive update.

Launch checklist: name the legal operator, provide a working privacy contact, verify hosting logs and processors, connect only services accurately described here, and add consent tools where legally required.
