Privacy policy

This policy explains the information involved when you visit Chiefletter or contact the editors.

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Who we are

Chiefletter is a technology publication. For privacy questions, contact contact@chiefletter.com.

Information involved in a visit

Chiefletter does not require an account and does not provide comments or a newsletter signup. The site uses Google Analytics and loads the Google AdSense tag. These services may receive data about page visits and browser requests according to their own settings and terms. Our hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, request time, browser type, and pages requested in ordinary server logs. The provider's retention and processing terms apply to those logs.

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash image services. Your browser makes requests to those services to display the pages. The site links to YouTube; it does not embed YouTube players. Each provider has its own privacy practices.

Article activity notes

Article pages include optional checklists and a note field. If you use them, your checklist progress and saved note are stored in local storage in your browser on your device. Chiefletter does not receive this note. Use the Clear activity button on the article to remove it, or clear this site’s browser storage. The note stays on this device unless your browser synchronizes its storage.

When you email us

If you choose to email Chiefletter, we receive the email address and information you include. We use it to respond, consider corrections or copyright notices, and maintain publication records. Email is handled by your and our email providers. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information by ordinary email.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

Google Analytics is loaded through the Google tag with measurement ID G-KL069686WS. The tag may collect page and browser information to measure site use. Google may use cookies or similar technologies according to the tag and account settings. The site also loads Google AdSense. Ad display, including Auto ads, depends on settings in the connected AdSense account. Google may receive a request when a page loads and process data under its own terms. Google has consent management requirements for some advertising uses and regions. We review the applicable requirements when configuring Analytics and advertising.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, privacy law may provide rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. To make a request, email contact@chiefletter.com. We may need to verify the request before acting. You can also manage external service requests directly with those providers.

Children's privacy

Chiefletter is a general-audience publication and is not designed to collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the site's operation changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
