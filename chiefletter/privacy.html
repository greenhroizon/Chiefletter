Privacy policy

This policy explains the information involved when you visit Chiefletter or contact the editors.

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Who we are

Chiefletter is a technology publication. For privacy questions, contact contact@chiefletter.com.

Information involved in a visit

Chiefletter does not require an account and does not provide comments or a newsletter signup. The site loads the Google tag script with measurement ID G-KL069686WS. The installed snippet initializes Google's data layer. Measurement behavior depends on the tag configuration. The site includes Google AdSense account verification metadata, but currently has no AdSense ad-serving script or ad units. Our hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, request time, browser type, and pages requested in ordinary server logs. The provider's retention and processing terms apply to those logs.

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts and photographs from Unsplash image services. Your browser makes requests to those services to display the pages. The site links to YouTube; it does not embed YouTube players. Each provider has its own privacy practices.

Browser-based tools and local storage

Some tools save a draft in your browser only when you choose Save draft. The code editor stores its HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in local storage so you can return to them. Use Reset example or clear this site’s browser storage to remove the draft. The image compressor and resizer process selected files in the browser and do not upload them to Chiefletter. A browser or extension may separately sync or inspect local data.

When you email us

If you choose to email Chiefletter, we receive the email address and information you include. We use it to respond, consider corrections or copyright notices, and maintain publication records. Email is handled by your and our email providers. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information by ordinary email.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The site requests the Google tag script with measurement ID G-KL069686WS. The current page snippet initializes the data layer; actual measurement depends on the Google tag configuration. Google’s script request may include technical browser and network information. The AdSense publisher ID appears in a verification meta tag. The site does not currently load the AdSense ad-serving script or display ad units. If advertising is enabled later, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated before ads are served. Review Google’s and your browser’s privacy controls for their separate practices.

Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, privacy law may provide rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. To make a request, email contact@chiefletter.com. We may need to verify the request before acting. You can also manage external service requests directly with those providers.

Children's privacy

Chiefletter is a general-audience publication and is not designed to collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the site's operation changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
