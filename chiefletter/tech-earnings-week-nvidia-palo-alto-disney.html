Earnings week: Nvidia, Palo Alto Networks and Disney headline the calendar

Federal Reserve meeting minutes also land Tuesday, adding a rates angle to the AI trade.

By ChiefLetter Editorial TeamPublished November 15, 20262 min read
Key takeaways
  • This week's earnings calendar is led by Nvidia, which reports after the market closes on Tuesday, November 17.
  • Tuesday is the busiest day.
  • Cybersecurity results from Palo Alto Networks offer a read on enterprise security budgets at a time when governments and companies are asking how AI changes software risk.

This week's earnings calendar is led by Nvidia, which reports after the market closes on Tuesday, November 17. Walt Disney and Canadian grocer Loblaw also report, according to BNN Bloomberg's week-ahead guide.

Tuesday is the busiest day. Palo Alto Networks, a major cybersecurity company, reports after the close, alongside Lowe's and TJX before the open. At 2 p.m. Eastern the Federal Reserve publishes minutes from its latest meeting.

The Fed minutes matter for technology stocks because high interest rates weigh on growth company valuations. Investors will scan the document for hints about the pace of future rate decisions.

Cybersecurity results from Palo Alto Networks offer a read on enterprise security budgets at a time when governments and companies are asking how AI changes software risk.

Check each company's investor relations page for exact times, which can change.
