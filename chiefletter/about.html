A note about this journal

Technology is learned one real problem at a time.

Plaintext is an independent publication for people finding their way into technology and for people trying to do thoughtful work once they get there.

What we publish

We write practical guides about learning technical skills, entering and growing in tech careers, and making everyday work clearer. We try to explain the trade-offs, name the limits of our advice, and leave readers with a next step they can actually try.

Our articles are written and edited for this site. We do not republish other publishers’ work. When we point to a video, documentation page, or outside resource, it should add context to the article rather than stand in for it.

How we work

Plaintext is an early-stage publication. The site currently identifies its author as the Plaintext Editors. Before public launch, the publisher should replace this collective byline with the real editor or contributor names, short biographies, experience, and a working contact address. We correct factual mistakes when they are brought to our attention and update time-sensitive guidance when it changes.

We aim to keep headlines accurate, distinguish observation from advice, and avoid promises about jobs, salaries, or guaranteed outcomes. Our content is general information and cannot account for every employer, jurisdiction, or individual circumstance.

Corrections and suggestions

If you spot a factual error, a broken link, or a topic we should cover, please use the contact page. Include the story title and the part you are writing about. We will review suggestions and make corrections when appropriate.
