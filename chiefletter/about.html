A note about this journal

Technology is learned one real problem at a time.

Chiefletter is an independent publication for people finding their way into technology and for people trying to do thoughtful work once they get there.

What we publish

We publish practical guides about learning technical skills, entering and growing in tech careers, and making everyday work clearer. Our aim is to explain trade-offs, give readers a concrete next step, and make complicated topics easier to approach.

Each article is organized around a real reader question. We include examples, checklists, FAQs, and links to further learning where they help readers act on the advice.

Our editorial approach

We aim for clear language, useful detail, and honest limits. Technical instructions and time-sensitive information should be checked against current documentation. We correct material errors and update articles when relevant tools or requirements change.

Chiefletter articles provide general education. They cannot account for every employer, jurisdiction, tool, or personal circumstance. They do not promise a job, salary, or specific career outcome.

Corrections and suggestions

Tell us what we should improve or cover next at contact@chiefletter.com. Include the article title and a link when writing about a correction.
