About ChiefLetter

ChiefLetter is an independent news site about the technology and business stories that shape how the world computes, communicates and competes. We publish concise, carefully sourced reporting on artificial intelligence infrastructure, semiconductors, regulation, space, robotics and the markets around them.

What we cover

Infrastructure

Data centers, power, cloud capacity and the economics of the AI buildout.

Policy

Elections, regulators and governments that decide what gets built and how.

Products and deals

Chips, games, launches, funding rounds and the transactions behind them.

How we work

Every article starts from primary sources, such as company announcements, official filings and government releases, and from reputable news reporting. We explain what happened, add the background a reader needs to understand it, and close with what to watch next. We list our sources at the end of each article so you can check them yourself.

When a report is unconfirmed, such as a leak or an anonymous-source story, we say so plainly. When facts change, we update the article and note it. Read the full details in our editorial policy.

How we are funded

ChiefLetter is supported by advertising. Advertisements are labelled and kept separate from our reporting, and advertisers do not influence what we write.
