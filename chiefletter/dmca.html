Copyright & DMCA

We respect intellectual property rights and review good-faith notices concerning material published on Chiefletter.

Send a copyright notice

Email contact@chiefletter.com with the subject “Copyright notice.” Include:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The Chiefletter page and material you want reviewed, with enough detail for us to locate it.
  • Your name and contact details.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent, or applicable law.
  • A statement that the information is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the rights holder.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

We may contact you for clarification and may share the notice with the person who submitted the material where appropriate. Do not include confidential information you do not want shared. Knowingly submitting a false claim may have legal consequences.

Counter-notice

If material you provided was removed because of a notice and you believe the removal was a mistake, email the same address with “Copyright counter-notice” in the subject. Identify the removed material and its former location, provide your contact details and signature, and include the statements required by the law that applies to your situation. Consider getting legal advice before submitting a counter-notice.

Image and other rights questions

For an image or other rights concern, identify the page and material. We will review the source and applicable license and take appropriate action where needed.

This page describes our contact process and is not legal advice. A notice sent to this email is not a representation that Chiefletter is a registered DMCA designated agent.
