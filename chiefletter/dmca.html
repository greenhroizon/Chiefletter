Copyright concerns

If you believe material on ChiefLetter infringes your copyright, send a notice to contact@chiefletter.com (configured in the supplied project; owner verification required). Include your signature, identification of the copyrighted work, the exact page and material at issue, your contact information, a good-faith statement that the use is not authorized, and a statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act.

Counter-notice

If material was removed or disabled and you believe that happened by mistake or misidentification, contact us at the same address with your signature, the material and former location, a statement under penalty of perjury explaining the mistake, and the legally required consent to jurisdiction and service of process. A counter-notice can have legal consequences.

Review and action

We will review complete notices and may remove or disable access to material while we assess the claim. We may forward a notice or counter-notice to the affected party where applicable. We do not promise a particular outcome or timetable.
