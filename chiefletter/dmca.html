Copyright & DMCA

We respect intellectual property rights and review good-faith notices concerning material published on this site.

Important setup: the publisher must add and monitor a real copyright contact address before launch. This page is informational and is not legal advice.

Submit a notice

If you believe material on this website infringes your copyright, send a notice to the designated agent at [INSERT MONITORED COPYRIGHT / DMCA EMAIL]. Do not publish until this placeholder is replaced. A notice should identify:

  • The copyrighted work you believe has been infringed, or a representative list if multiple works are involved.
  • The specific material on this site and enough information to locate it.
  • Your contact information, including a mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A statement that you have a good-faith belief the disputed use is not authorized by the rights holder, its agent, or law.
  • A statement, made under penalty of perjury where applicable, that the information is accurate and you are authorized to act for the rights holder.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

We may forward a notice to the person who supplied the material where applicable. Do not include confidential information you do not want shared.

Counter-notification

If material you provided was removed or disabled because of a notice and you believe the removal was mistaken, you may submit a counter-notification through the same verified contact route. Include the information required by applicable law, identify the material and its former location, provide your contact details and signature, and make the required statements about good faith, jurisdiction, and accepting service of process. Seek legal advice if you are unsure about your rights or obligations.

Repeat infringement

The publisher should maintain a process for reviewing valid notices and taking appropriate action, including termination of repeat infringers where required by law. The publisher must confirm whether it needs to register a designated agent and keep that information current before relying on a DMCA process.

Other rights concerns

For image licensing questions, identify the page and image. We will review the source and applicable license. Do not submit false or misleading claims; legal consequences may apply.
