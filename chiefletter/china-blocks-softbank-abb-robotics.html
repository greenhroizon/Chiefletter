China blocks SoftBank's acquisition of ABB's robotics business

Beijing's decision halts one of the largest pending deals in industrial robotics.

China has blocked SoftBank's planned acquisition of ABB's robotics business, according to Silicon UK's November coverage. The deal would have placed one of the industry's best-known robot makers under SoftBank's control.

The block shows how regulatory approval in China can decide the fate of cross-border technology deals, especially in strategic sectors such as automation and AI-linked hardware.

Merger approval in China is a gate for global deals when the target sells there. Regulators can block, delay or demand conditions, and strategic sectors such as robotics get close attention.

For SoftBank, which has pushed hard into AI and robotics, the decision closes one route to building a physical-AI portfolio and may push it toward partnerships or smaller acquisitions.

For the wider industry, it is a reminder that factory automation sits at the intersection of technology policy and trade tension.
