Nvidia earnings preview: what to expect from the November 17 report

Wall Street expects roughly 90% growth, and options traders are pricing a bigger-than-usual move.

Key takeaways

  • Analysts forecast revenue and earnings per share rising about 90% from a year earlier, according to a MarketPulse preview.
  • Beyond the headline figures, investors will listen for confirmation of the fiscal 2028 growth target, any update on export restrictions to China, and management comments on the leadership questions covered in our related story.

Nvidia reports its fiscal third-quarter results on Tuesday, November 17, at about 4:20 p.m. Eastern time, with a conference call at 5 p.m. It is the biggest scheduled test of the AI trade this week, and the stock has been unusually volatile in the run-up.

Also read: Nvidia loses about $190 billion in value after reports of a probe tied to CEO Jensen Huang

Analysts forecast revenue and earnings per share rising about 90% from a year earlier, according to a MarketPulse preview. Company guidance pointed to roughly $108 billion in revenue and a gross margin near 74%. For comparison, Nvidia reported $57.0 billion in revenue for the same quarter a year ago.

Options markets implied a move of about 10.6% in either direction as of November 10. That is above the 7.8% average peak earnings-day move of the past ten years, MarketPulse reported, a sign traders expect a sharp reaction whichever way the numbers land.

Beyond the headline figures, investors will listen for confirmation of the fiscal 2028 growth target, any update on export restrictions to China, and management comments on the leadership questions covered in our related story. Guidance for the next quarter usually moves the stock as much as the quarter just reported.

This is news coverage, not investment advice. Results can differ from forecasts, and figures here come from published market previews and company statements.
