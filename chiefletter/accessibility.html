Our commitment

Accessibility

Chiefletter aims to make its articles readable and usable across devices, input methods, and assistive technologies.

What we work toward

We use semantic page structure, descriptive headings and links, responsive layouts, and alt text for editorial images. We aim for keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, and pages that remain usable when text is enlarged.

Report a barrier

If you have trouble reading or navigating a page, email contact@chiefletter.com. Please share the page URL, what you were trying to do, and the device or assistive technology involved if you are comfortable doing so. We will review the report and work to address the issue.
