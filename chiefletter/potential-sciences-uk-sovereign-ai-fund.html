High-quality scientific data is increasingly seen as the bottleneck for AI in chemistry and materials, which makes data-focused startups a growing investment target.

Chemistry has plenty of published results but far fewer consistent, machine-readable data sets. Models trained on messy data struggle to predict how molecules behave.
