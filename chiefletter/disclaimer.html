Plaintext publishes general information and practical learning material about technology work.

Not individualized advice

Our articles are not legal, financial, employment, cybersecurity, medical, or other professional advice. Laws, hiring practices, tools, and technical requirements vary by place and change over time. Technical use

Code, workflows, and tool suggestions are provided for educational purposes. Test changes in a safe environment, maintain backups, and follow your organization's security and data policies. Do not test systems unless you own them or have explicit authorization.

Career articles describe approaches, not guaranteed outcomes. We do not promise a job, salary, promotion, interview, certification result, or business result. Your circumstances, qualifications, location, and the market all matter.

Links to third-party websites, YouTube searches, documentation, and services are provided for convenience. We do not control their availability or practices. The publisher must disclose any future sponsorship, affiliate relationship, or paid placement clearly and near the relevant recommendation. At present this starter site contains no affiliate disclosures or paid endorsements.

We aim to correct material errors and review time-sensitive articles. Report a concern through our contact page.
