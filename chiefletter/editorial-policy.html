Editorial Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026.

ChiefLetter publishes technology guides and explainers intended to help readers understand a topic and take practical next steps. Clear sourcing, useful context and honest limits matter more to us than a claim that an article is “SEO optimized.”

Accuracy and sources

For factual claims, we consult product documentation, primary documents and reliable reporting where appropriate. We distinguish confirmed information from interpretation, avoid overstating what a tool can do, and review time-sensitive details before publication. Guides should explain when readers need to check current manufacturer or service instructions.

Originality and editorial review

Articles should be written in original language and add practical explanation rather than copy another publisher’s work. Editing tools, including AI-assisted tools, may support research or drafting. A named editor is responsible for checking claims, links, clarity and usefulness before publication; automated output is not a substitute for that review. The publisher should identify the responsible editor on this page before launch.

Dates and updates

Publication dates are shown on guides. We revise pages when a meaningful change improves accuracy or usefulness and should update the visible date and structured metadata when an article is materially revised. The owner should confirm that the staged publishing schedule reflects real editorial publication history before launch.

Independence and advertising

Advertising or sponsorship should not determine an article’s conclusions. Paid placements and affiliate relationships, if introduced, must be clearly identified. The current build includes an AdSense loader but no ad units or placements.

Corrections

If you spot a factual error, email contact@chiefletter.com with the page URL, the statement and supporting information. This address is listed in the supplied project and must be verified by the owner before publication. We will review substantiated corrections and note significant changes where appropriate.

Scope

Our content is general information, not individualized legal, financial, medical or security advice. Readers should consider their own situation and consult a qualified professional where needed.
