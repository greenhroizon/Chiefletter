How we publish

Editorial standards

The aim is useful guidance written with care: specific enough to help, honest enough to trust, and open to correction.

Originality and authorship

Articles should be created for Plaintext, reviewed for clarity and accuracy, and attributed to real contributors with relevant experience. Before launch, replace the starter “Plaintext Editors” byline with accurate author biographies and an accountable editor. Do not present generated drafts or unverified claims as firsthand reporting.

Research and sourcing

We prefer primary sources such as official documentation, standards, and original research for factual claims. Articles should link to sources when a reader would benefit from verification, especially for changing products, policies, or security practices. Keyword choices are based on reader questions and search intent; no search-volume data is claimed in this starter content.

Review and updates

Technical instructions should be reviewed in their intended context before publication. Time-sensitive pages should show a review date and be revisited when tools or requirements change. Corrections should be made clearly and promptly when an error could mislead a reader.

Recommendations and conflicts

Any future advertising, sponsorship, affiliate relationship, or material conflict must be disclosed clearly near the content it affects. Payment will not purchase an undisclosed favorable review. We do not currently operate affiliate links in this starter site.

Images and video

Image rights and license conditions must be checked for each selected image and retained by the publisher. Descriptive alt text should explain relevant image content. Video links should support the surrounding original explanation and not replace it.

Corrections

Send a note via the contact page, including the story and issue. We will review it and update the article when appropriate.
