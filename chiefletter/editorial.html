How we publish

Editorial standards

Our aim is useful guidance written with care: specific enough to help, clear about its limits, and open to correction.

Originality and accuracy

Chiefletter publishes articles prepared for this publication. We aim to explain technical ideas in our own words, distinguish practical suggestions from established facts, and avoid promises about jobs, salaries, or guaranteed outcomes. We check technical details against reliable sources and update material when it becomes inaccurate or outdated.

Research and sources

We prefer primary sources such as official documentation, standards, and original research for factual claims. Links should give readers a useful path to verify details or learn more. Search terms help us understand the questions people ask; they do not replace reporting, analysis, or a clear answer.

Corrections and updates

If you find an error or broken link, email contact@chiefletter.com with the article title and relevant passage. We review correction requests and update articles when appropriate.

Recommendations and conflicts

Any sponsorship, affiliate relationship, or paid placement will be identified near the relevant recommendation. Payment will not determine an undisclosed favorable review.

Images and accessibility

We use images from Unsplash and provide descriptive alternative text. We aim to make pages keyboard-accessible, responsive, and readable with assistive technology. See our accessibility statement to report a barrier.
