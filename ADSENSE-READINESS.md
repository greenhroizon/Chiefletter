# AdSense and publication checklist

The AdSense loader and Google Analytics tag `G-KL069686WS` are installed in the site files. No manual ad units are configured. Auto ads depend on the AdSense account settings. Approval is decided by Google and cannot be guaranteed by a site checklist.

- Verify the live domain in the AdSense application flow and ensure the site is publicly accessible over HTTPS.
- Review every article for accuracy, usefulness, originality, and current technical details. Add primary source links where readers need to verify changing claims.
- Keep navigation clear, pages functional, and ads visually separate from navigation and article controls.
- Keep the Privacy and Cookie pages aligned with actual hosting, analytics, contact, newsletter, and advertising services.
- If serving personalized Google ads in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, Google currently requires a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF. Check the live policy and certified CMP list during setup.
- Retain source and license information for images and disclose any future sponsorship or affiliate relationship near the relevant recommendation.
