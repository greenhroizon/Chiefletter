What this model shows

A battery meter estimates remaining usable charge from voltage, current, temperature, and a learned model. Follow each stage in order. The moving marker represents the part of the process receiving attention, not a literal object in every system.

Where the simple picture has limits

A drop from 20 percent to zero is not a precise fuel gauge reading. Cell age, cold weather, and heavy load can change the estimate.
